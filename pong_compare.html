What to compare while both are running

  • Whether progress looks smooth or noisy as generations and episodes accumulate.
  • How quickly each method produces a stable paddle policy under the same playback speed.
  • Which charts feel most intuitive for explaining learning to a new viewer: fitness trends or reward/value trends.
  • How often you want to use native app controls instead of synchronized commands while investigating a specific behavior.

Shared-control mapping

  • Advance both once triggers Evolution’s Train 1 generation and RL’s Next +1 ep.
  • Advance both in batches triggers Evolution’s Next +10 gen and RL’s Next +10 ep so the shared controls advance both apps more evenly.
  • Playback speed sets both embedded apps’ speed sliders to the same value.
  • Matched cadence now pushes the same numeric ticks-per-second value into both apps instead of scaling from different max-rate sliders.
